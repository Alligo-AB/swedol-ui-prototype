Tag

A tag shows an active filter above a product list or a search result. Its × button removes the filter.

Tokens, sizes, measurements, states and requirements. Values come from the live token package and from Figma: Tag.

Accessibility Requirements

TopicRequirement
SemanticsThe tag is text, never a button or a link. The × is a native <button type="button">.
NameThe × has aria-label="Remove <label>" (WCAG 4.1.2).
FocusWhen the × has :focus-visible, a ring is drawn around the whole tag (WCAG 2.4.7). After removing, focus moves to a neighbour, never to the page start (WCAG 2.4.3).
Target sizeThe × is 16px. Leave space around it, so the target with its spacing reaches 24×24px (WCAG 2.5.8).
