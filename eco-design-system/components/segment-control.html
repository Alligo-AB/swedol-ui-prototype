Pill segment control

A pill segment control switches between two to four related views or filters in the same surface. A pill slides to the active segment.

Every example on this page is the real component, not a picture. Specification: .claude/skills/eco-pill-segment-control/SKILL.md. Figma: Pill segment control.

Playground

Click a segment and the pill slides to it. Large is the page-level switcher; XSmall is for tight toolbars. Below 640px the control fills its parent; from 640px it shrinks to its content.

Variants
Four sizes, two to four segments. The pill is black by default; use the white pill when the control already sits on a dark or grey surface.
Sizes
White pill
For a control inside another, larger control, on a grey track. The active text stays black.


States
Hover darkens the track to border-tertiary and underlines the inactive segment. Focus has no style of its own: the buttons use the global keyboard focus ring. Hover is forced on this page.

Do
  • Use for two to four related views in one surface.
  • Choose the size by where it sits.
  • Listen for segment-change to swap the content.
  • Keep labels short.
Don’t
  • Replace tabs or radio buttons with it.
  • Add a second focus ring.
  • Let it wrap onto two rows.
  • Build your own thumb positioning: use the shared script.
