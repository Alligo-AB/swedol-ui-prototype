Banner

A banner carries a page-wide or system-level message: an outage, planned maintenance, a campaign or a notice to one customer group. It sits at the top and stays until it is closed or the situation changes.

How to build a banner and the CSS behind it. The files are eco-design-system/notifications.css and notifications.js.

Examples & GuidelinesSpecificationsCode

Basic Usage

Link the shared files once per page. Never copy the rules into a page.

StepWhat to do
1. Link<link rel="stylesheet" href="/eco-design-system/notifications.css"> and <script src="/eco-design-system/notifications.js"></script>. Link button.css first. The script makes the × work.
2. MarkupCopy a recipe below. Put the banner at the top of the content.
3. ChooseStatus class, emphasis class (weak is the default), size (Small, or --large on My Pages) or Promotion.
4. Close buttonLeave out the × when the banner may not be closed.

Banner

The markup has no motion. The closing animation is set in Motion on Examples & Guidelines.

API

Classes

ClassUse
banner-notification--{info|success|warning|error}The status.
banner-notification--{strong|weak|weaker}The emphasis.
banner-notification--largeLarge (My Pages only).
banner-notification--promotion and --{dark|light|accent-strong|accent-weak}Promotion.

Custom properties and attributes

NameUse
--n-close-ease, --n-close-durationClosing motion. Defaults: ease-accelerate-generic, duration-fast-3.
data-n-close="fade"On the banner or an ancestor: fade only, no height collapse.
n-dismissedEvent, bubbles, fired when the banner is hidden.

Things to know

  • The × is the Close icon button; the script hides the banner with hidden.
  • A Promotion banner takes its accent from the brand tokens: do not hardcode a color.
  • Buttons inside a banner are always 32px high, also when button.css is on the page.
