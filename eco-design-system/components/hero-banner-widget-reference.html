Hero banner

The Hero banner is a versatile component for campaign features and for the headers of landing pages, on campaign pages and sites and on brand pages. It has many settings for building responsive hero sections.

Finished banners to learn from, with the type, the purpose and the elements that make each one clear.

Use cases and banner reference examples

The examples below are for inspiration and as a reference for how to use the hero banner. Each one shows what kind of banner it is, what it is for, which elements carry the message and which e-commerce category it belongs to.

error

As a rule of thumb, use the component consistently across similar page types, for example custom headers on brand landing pages.

Campaign banner with a product focus

This banner is an example of a campaign banner with a product focus.

Type of banner

  • Campaign banner / hero banner: highlights one product, the WR36DE impact wrench from Hikoki.
  • Discount banner: shows a clear price reduction (25% off), which makes it campaign oriented.
  • Power Deal banner: the text POWER DEAL signals a special promotion.

Purpose

  • Drive sales through the discount: a clear price drop, from 3,500 SEK to 2,625 SEK.
  • Highlight product benefits: a short description of the features (brushless motor, longer runtime, longer life, less maintenance).
  • Conversion oriented: two clear buttons, “Shop now” for a direct purchase and “View campaign” for more offers in the same campaign.

Elements that make this clear

  • Price and discount: large red text with the discount and the new price.
  • Product image in use: the tool in a real work environment adds credibility.
  • Campaign branding: the POWER DEAL logo and the Hikoki logo give the campaign a strong identity.
  • Color coding: green and yellow create energy and draw attention.

Category in e-commerce

  • Campaign banner (discounts and special offers)
  • Conversion-focused banner (clear calls to action)
  • Product-focused banner (a specific product with price and features)

Product banner with a discount offer

This banner is an example of a product banner with a discount offer.

Type of banner

  • Product banner / hero banner: highlights one product, the AmPro tool bag.
  • Discount banner: “20% off” and the new price (499 SEK) show that the purpose is to sell with an offer.
  • Brand banner: connected to AmPro, which reinforces the brand identity.

Purpose

  • Drive direct sales of one product: the button “Shop now” leads to the product page.
  • Create a reason to buy: 20% off and clear pricing are strong motivators.
  • Highlight product features: a short description of the material, the handle and the detachable shoulder strap.

Elements that make this clear

  • Background and layout: a black background with the product in focus gives a premium feel.
  • Headline and subtext: the headline names the product, the subtext describes its function and material.
  • Discount highlight: a red label with “20% off” and the price in large text.
  • CTA button: “Shop now”, conversion focused.
  • Brand logo: the AmPro logo top right for clear identity.

Category in e-commerce

  • Product banner (one product)
  • Discount or campaign banner (to drive sales with an offer)
  • Brand banner (linked to AmPro)

Product and new product campaign banner

This banner is an example of a product campaign banner.

Type of banner

  • Hero banner / product banner: draws attention to one product, here an AmPro tool bag.
  • New product banner: a clear label with the text “NEW!” shows that the product is new in the range.

Purpose

  • Promote a new product: inform visitors about a new product and create interest.
  • Conversion oriented: two clear buttons, “Shop now” for a direct purchase and “More about the product” for further information.

Elements that make this clear

  • Product image: a clear image of the bag shows what is sold.
  • Product name and description: clear and short, the name and a short description of material and features.

Category in e-commerce

  • Promotional banner (campaigns, new arrivals or launches)
  • Conversion-focused banner (clear calls to action)
  • Product-focused banner (a specific product, not a general brand banner)

Seasonal campaign banner with a category focus

This banner is an example of a seasonal campaign banner with a category focus.

Type of banner

  • Seasonal banner / hero banner: promotes products for a specific season, here spring.
  • Category overview: shows a whole category (lawnmowers, leaf blowers, spades, saws and more) instead of one product.
  • Inspirational banner: encourages customers to prepare for gardening and watering.

Purpose

  • Drive traffic to category pages: the banner leads customers to explore a range, not to buy one product.
  • Create seasonal relevance: “Equip yourself for spring” connects to what customers need at this time of year.
  • Broad conversion focus: the button “Equip yourself now” leads to a collection page.

Elements that make this clear

  • Background image: a garden hose on grass signals gardening and watering.
  • Headline and subtext: a clear seasonal call to action, and a line that there are many new tools and machines for pros and private persons.
  • Listed product categories: lawn mowers, leaf blowers, pest control, spades, rakes, saws.
  • CTA button: leads to a broader category, not a single product.

Category in e-commerce

  • Seasonal banner (drive sales in a specific period)
  • Category or assortment banner (several products within a theme)
  • Inspirational banner (creates need rather than selling one item)

Clearance sale banner with a campaign focus

This banner is an example of a clearance sale banner with a campaign focus.

Type of banner

  • Clearance banner / hero banner: communicates a major stock clearance.
  • Discount banner: “Up to 80% off” attracts attention with big price reductions.
  • Category banner: names several categories (tools, clothing, shoes, safety gear, electrical).

Purpose

  • Drive traffic to the clearance page: the button “Go to clearance sale” leads to a collection page with discounted products.
  • Create urgency: the campaign period (3–20 Nov) and “while supplies last”.
  • Reduce excess stock: the banner explains that the products are overstocked or being discontinued.

Elements that make this clear

  • Background image: tools in rows give a sense of a wide range.
  • Headline and subtext: a strong clearance signal, and why the discounts exist and how long the campaign lasts.
  • Discount highlight: “Up to 80% off” in red.
  • CTA button: “Go to clearance sale”, conversion focused.

Category in e-commerce

  • Clearance banner (stock reduction and big discounts)
  • Campaign banner (with a clear time limit)
  • Category or assortment banner (several product groups)

Brand awareness and new product hero

This banner is an example of a product campaign with brand awareness.

Type of banner

  • New product banner / hero banner: introduces a new product line, HiVis from Björnkläder.
  • Brand banner: strongly tied to one brand (Björnkläder), and highlights a product series.
  • Category or collection banner: focuses on a whole collection of high-visibility workwear, not one product.

Purpose

  • Inform about the launch and drive traffic to the collection: the button “Explore the collection” leads to a page with the full range.
  • Position the product as premium and safety oriented: the text stresses maximum visibility and safety at the workplace.
  • Build brand identity: through the tagline “Engineered to stand out”.

Elements that make this clear

  • Background image: a worker in high-visibility clothing at height signals safety and function.
  • Headline and subtext: clear brand positioning, and a line that the collection is made for visibility and safety.
  • New product marker: a yellow circle with “New!” draws attention.
  • CTA button: “Explore the collection”, conversion focused.

Category in e-commerce

  • New product banner (launch of new products or collections)
  • Brand banner (linked to Björnkläder)
  • Category or collection banner (a whole series of products)

Brand landing page hero

This banner is an example of a brand landing page hero.

Type of banner

  • Brand banner / hero banner: clearly linked to the AmPro brand, and shows its products.
  • Category or assortment banner: focuses on tools and storage solutions, not one product.
  • Inspirational banner: communicates value and quality instead of a campaign or discount.

Purpose

  • Build brand identity and drive traffic to the AmPro range: the button “Shop AmPro” leads to a collection page with all the products of the brand.
  • Position the products as premium and functional: the text “Tools and storage that make a difference” signals quality and utility.
  • Create trust and interest: a tagline that stresses flexibility and solutions.

Elements that make this clear

  • Background image: a close-up of a tool drawer with neatly arranged wrenches conveys order, quality and precision.
  • Headline and subtext: strong brand positioning, and a line that reinforces the value of the brand.
  • CTA button: “Shop AmPro”, conversion focused.
  • Brand logo: the AmPro logo bottom right for clear identity.

Category in e-commerce

  • Brand banner (strengthens the brand profile)
  • Category or assortment banner (a whole product category)
  • Inspirational banner (creates interest rather than selling one product)
