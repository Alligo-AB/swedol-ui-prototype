Components · Notifications

E-Com Toast

An E-Com Toast confirms a shopping action without leaving the page: a product added to the cart, or saved to the wishlist. It has no status color; it speaks in the store’s own black and white.

Tokens, sizes, measurements, states and requirements. Values come from the live token package and from E-Com Toast.
