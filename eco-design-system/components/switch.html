Switch

A switch turns one setting on or off and applies the change at once. It is for a single binary choice, not for picking between options.

Every example on this page is the real component. Click, tab and press Space to try it. Specification: .claude/skills/eco-switch/SKILL.md.

Sizes

    
Large and Small have the same 48×24px track and differ only in label size. X-Small (class .form-switch-item--xs) has a 32×16px track for dense layouts.

    

    
Settings list

    
A switch applies its change at once, so a list of settings needs no Save button. The label names the setting; the switch shows the state, so never change the label to “On” or “Off”.

    

    
Without a visible label

    
Only when the setting is named by its surroundings, for example a row in a table. It needs an aria-label.

Anatomy & specs

    


The track is a pill (border-radius: 999px) and the thumb moves by transform. The focus ring is 2px with a 2px offset around the track. The gap to the label is 8px in every size.

Usage

    


Switch, checkbox, radio and segment control look alike but do different jobs. Choose by what the user is deciding.

    
The user…Use
turns one setting on or off, with immediate effectSwitch
picks any number of options, saved on submiteco-checkbox
picks exactly one of several optionseco-radio
switches between two related views or filterseco-pill-segment-control

    

      
Do
  • Describe the setting in the label, in sentence case.
  • Apply the change immediately.
  • Use .form-switch-item--dark on dark surfaces.
  • Test Space, Tab and label click.

      
Don’t
  • Pair a switch with a Save button for the same setting.
  • Change the label text to “On” or “Off”.
  • Use a switch to choose between options.
  • Use the dark markup on a light surface.
