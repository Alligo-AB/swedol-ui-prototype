Tokens, sizes and requirements. Values come from Figma: Dot.

Position on an iconThe center of the dot sits on the top right corner of the icon

A dot has no padding, so there is no measurement figure. Diameter values are from Figma (Dot).

Spacing

The dot has no spacing of its own. The page sets the gap to the label or the position on the icon.

States

A dot has no interaction states. Its state is the status it shows: Danger, Warning, Success, Information or Neutral.

Behavior

TopicRule
InteractionNone. Never a link or a button.
