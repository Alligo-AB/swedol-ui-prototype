Switch

A switch turns one setting on or off and applies the change at once. It is for a single binary choice, not for picking between options.

Tokens, sizes, measurements, states and requirements. Values come from the live token package and from Figma: Toggle Switch.

Behavior

InputResult
Click switch or labelToggles on and off, and the change applies at once.
SpaceToggles the focused switch.
TabThe ring shows on keyboard focus only (:focus-visible), for both off and on.
MarkupA native input type="checkbox" role="switch" inside a label. Never a div or button imitation.
DisabledNot focusable. Muted track, no thumb shadow, text-disabled label.
MotionReduced motion turns the transition off.

Accessibility Requirements

TopicRequirement
ElementA native <input type="checkbox" role="switch"> in a <label>. The name, the role and the on/off state are announced.
NameA visible label, or aria-label when the setting is named by its surroundings.
FocusRing with border-focus, 2px, offset 2px, on :focus-visible (WCAG 2.4.7, 1.4.11).
ColorOn and off differ in the position of the thumb as well as the track color (WCAG 1.4.1).
EffectThe change applies at once. A switch that needs a Save button is a checkbox.
MotionReduced motion turns the transition off (WCAG 2.3.3).
DisabledNot focusable. Disabled tokens are exempt from contrast requirements.
