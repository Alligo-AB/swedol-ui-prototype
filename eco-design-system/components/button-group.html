Button group

A button group is a row of joined System buttons for choosing between two to four short options. The selected button has a black 12% fill.

Every example on this page is the real component, not a picture. Specification: .claude/skills/eco-button/SKILL.md.

Usage
The user…Use
picks one of 2–4 short options on a toolbar or in a playgroundButton group
switches the main view of a surface with a sliding pillPill segment control
picks one of many optionseco-select
picks one and submitseco-radio
Do
  • Keep to 2–4 options with short labels.
  • Label the group with aria-label or a visible label.
  • Set aria-pressed on the selected button.
  • Link button-group.css.
Don’t
  • Wrap the group onto two rows.
  • Use it for page navigation.
  • Allow more than one selected unless you mean a multi-select toggle.
  • Copy its rules into the page.
