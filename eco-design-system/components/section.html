Section

A section is a full-width block of a page. Its padding adjusts at each breakpoint and its surface is white or grey.

Every example on this page is the real component, not a picture. Specification: .claude/skills/eco-section/SKILL.md.

Surface and sides
PartRule
Surfacesurface-raised-primary (white, default) or surface-raised-secondary (grey). A page starts with white or uses white only; grey marks a clear change. No other backgrounds without design approval.
Sidesvar(--px-full): the grid margin (16 / 32 / 40px) that also centers content against the 1200px maximum width.
PaddingNormally always on. It is the white space that divides content.


Zeroed padding
Turn the built-in padding off only in these five cases (.section--flush), and with a clear purpose.
#CaseUse when
1Divider inside a sectionTwo parts with the same background, no clear break wanted, but the content should keep the section padding.
2Adjacent topic with an in-between sectionTwo sections cover one topic and a semi-topic section (cross-sell, product placement) sits between them.
3Fluid hero bannerThe section is the container of a full-width banner for new products or a campaign.
4First section next to a component with built-in spacingZero the top padding when the content follows a breadcrumb.
5First section with a fluid banner firstZero top and bottom when the page starts with a full-width banner or image.


Page divider
A thin line between two sections of the same color, when a full section break would feel heavy. It is a div, not a section, and uses --px-full so the line stops where the content stops.
Replacing a section boundary
With its own 48px top and bottom padding.
Between two sections that already have padding
Zero the divider’s own vertical padding, or the air stacks.

Do
  • Write the xs padding first, then add sm, md, lg and xl.
  • Mark the first section with .section--first.
  • Use --px-full for the sides.
  • Zero the padding only for the five cases.
Don’t
  • Use another background without design approval.
  • Use --px-page for the divider’s sides.
  • Stack a divider’s 48px on a section’s own padding.
  • Use zeroed padding without a clear purpose.
