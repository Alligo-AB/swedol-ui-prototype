Collapsible

A collapsible is a row that opens and closes to show more content, used for FAQ lists. The content animates open.

Every example on this page is the real component, not a picture. Specification: .claude/skills/eco-collapsible/SKILL.md.

Usage
The user…Use
skims a list of questions or sections and opens one at a timeCollapsible
must see all the textPlain text with headings
switches between views of the same contenteco-pill-segment-control
moves between pagesTabs or navigation
Do
  • Use for FAQ and long, optional content.
  • Keep titles short and written as the question or topic.
  • Hide the last row’s line when the list ends the section.
  • Use min-height, not height, on the header.
Don’t
  • Hide content people must read to continue.
  • Nest collapsibles.
  • Put actions or forms in the header.
  • Animate with CSS height: it cannot reach auto.
