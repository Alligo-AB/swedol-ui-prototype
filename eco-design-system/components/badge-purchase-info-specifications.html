Badge Purchase Info

A purchase info badge is a badge with a beak that introduces a short purchase tip.

Tokens, sizes and requirements. Values come from the live token package and from Figma: Good buy, Good to know.

States

The badge has no interaction states. Links in the text have the states of an inline link.

Behavior

TopicRule
InteractionNone on the badge. Links in the text are normal links.
WrappingThe text wraps beside the badge; the badge keeps its size.
BeakAlways points right, to the text.

Accessibility Requirements

TopicRequirement
NameLabel and text, read in order. The beak is decorative (empty).
ColorNot the only carrier of meaning: the label says “Bra köp” or “Bra att veta”.
ContrastWhite on red or on surface-50 (#737373, 4.7:1). Text text-secondary on white.
LinksUnderlined, keyboard reachable, visible focus.
