Tile link

A tile link is a clickable, graphical link with an SVG graphic or an icon with a label. It highlights navigation destinations in grids and horizontal scrollers.

Tokens, sizes, measurements, states and requirements. Values come from the live token package and from Figma: Tile link.

States

Hover is forced on this page; in your code it comes from :hover.

Brand (SVG)

  • Enabled: a White container with a subtle border and the logo at full contrast; or a Black container with no visible border and the logo at full contrast.
  • Hover, White: the border appears or darkens (border-hover, 1px). The logo is unchanged.
  • Hover, Black: the surface is surface-action-1 with surface-opacity-white-20 on top. The logo is unchanged.

Label + icon and Label only

  • Enabled: a White or Grey container with a subtle border and the content at full contrast.
  • Hover: the border appears or darkens (border-hover, 1px), and the label and the icon shift to the action hover color.

Behavior

InputResult
Click anywhere on the tileFollows the link.
Enter, TabFollows the link; 2px focus ring on keyboard focus.
HoverThe border darkens. A Label + icon or Label only tile also changes the color of the label and the icon; a brand logo is unchanged. The Black tile gets a 20% white overlay.
MarkupThe whole tile is one <a href>. Brand tiles need an alt with the brand name.

Accessibility Requirements

TopicRequirement
ElementOne <a href> for the whole tile. Links navigate; buttons act.
NameThe label is the link text. On a brand tile the alt of the logo is the link text (WCAG 1.1.1, 2.4.4).
FocusA 2px ring with border-focus and 2px offset, on keyboard focus (WCAG 2.4.7, 1.4.11).
ContrastThe border and text meet the contrast requirements on White, Grey and Black. Use the white logo version on the Black tile.
