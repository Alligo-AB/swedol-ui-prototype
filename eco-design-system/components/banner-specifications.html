Components · Notifications

Banner

A banner carries a page-wide or system-level message: an outage, planned maintenance, a campaign or a notice to one customer group. It sits at the top and stays until it is closed or the situation changes.

Tokens, sizes, measurements, states and requirements. Values come from the live token package and from Banner.

Behavior

InputResult
Click ×Hides the banner. Whether the × exists is decided by the banner’s owner.
PersistenceA banner never disappears by itself. It stays until it is closed or the state changes.
TabThe ×, buttons and links take focus with a visible ring.
Inline linkUnderlined when enabled; on hover the underline goes and the color changes (eco-inline-link, Medium; inverted on Strong and Promotion Dark). Brand-accent Promotion keeps its text color on hover.
OrderPlace the banner at the top of the content in the DOM so screen reader users meet it first.

Accessibility Requirements

TopicRequirement
MarkupA Strong or Error banner for an outage should use role="alert"; other banners need no role.
StatusThe icon and the text carry the status, not the color alone (WCAG 1.4.1).
FocusThe ×, buttons and links have a 2px ring, border-focus, on keyboard use (WCAG 2.4.7).
ContrastStrong uses white text on the solid status color; Weak and Weaker use text-primary.
Reduced motionThe banner is hidden without a transition under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.
