Hero banner

The Hero banner is a versatile component for campaign features and for the headers of landing pages, on campaign pages and sites and on brand pages. It has many settings for building responsive hero sections.

The anatomy, the sizes and the colors of the hero banner. Figma: Main hero content, Power Deal, Brand logos and News badge.

DesignUsageReference and ExamplesStoryblok

Anatomy

The hero banner combines an image, typography, campaign elements and call-to-action buttons into a high-impact page header. Every part is optional except the image and the title.

Main hero content

  • 1

    Background image. An image for a campaign, or the header of a brand or campaign page. On a Fluid hero it fills to the left and right edge of the viewport.

  • 2*

    Brand logo, in content. For brand identity. *Only for brand pages, when the content is centred in breakpoint XSmall.

  • 3a

    Discount badge. A red badge with the discount or a short campaign message.

  • 3b

    Campaign price. The promotional price, next to the badge.

  • 3c

    Old price info. The price before the discount.

  • 4a

    Pre title. Categorises the content or adds context.

  • 4b

    Title. The headline or campaign message.

  • 4c

    Preamble. A longer description of the campaign, product or brand.

  • 5

    Buttons. Compelling calls to action. One primary and, optionally, one secondary button.

  • 6

    Visual enhancement overlay. A gradient that keeps the text readable on the image.

Brand elements

Brand elements are placed on the image, never in the text column. They are the Power Deal concept, brand logos and the news badge.

  • 1

    Power Deal border. A graphic that is part of the Power Deal concept. Use it only together with the Power Deal logo.

  • 2

    Power Deal logo. The logo of the Power Deal concept. Always use it together with the Power Deal border to strengthen the concept. It can sit top left, top right or bottom right.

  • 1

    Brand logo. For brand identity or a campaign-specific product logo. It can sit top right, bottom right or in the content.

  • 1

    News badge. A circular badge that promotes new products and other news.

Colors

Branding elements

Power Deal border

The color depends on the site where the campaign is shown. Swedol uses its accent color and Tools uses its own. The site decides, so the editor never picks it. Figma: Power Deal border.

Power Deal logo

The logo follows the same rule: the slash takes the accent color of the site. Figma: Power Deal logo.

News badge

The text “NYHET!” follows the language of the country. The badge is only for promoting news. White and black work on both brands. Green belongs to Swedol and red to Tools. Figma: News badge.

Text elements

Text uses Primary (black) or Primary inverted (white). Choose the one that is most readable on the image, and test it with the overlay.

Buttons

The button group uses Primary and Secondary on a light image, and Primary inverted and Secondary inverted on a dark one. See Button.
