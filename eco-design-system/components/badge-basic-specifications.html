Badge Basic

A basic badge is a read-only status or label. It is never clickable.

Tokens, sizes, measurements and requirements. Values come from the live token package and from Figma: Basic.

Sizes

Three sizes. Large and Medium look different on mobile and desktop. Small is the same size as Medium on mobile. Padding is under Measurements.

States

A badge has no interaction states: no hover, no focus, no pressed, no disabled.

Behavior

TopicRule
InteractionNone. A badge is never a link or a button, has no focus and no hover state.
WidthHugs the text. No maximum: keep labels to one or two words.
WrappingNever wraps (white-space: nowrap).
