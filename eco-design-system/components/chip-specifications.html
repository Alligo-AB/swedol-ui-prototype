Chip

A chip is a small, interactive element that represents an input, an attribute or an action, most often a filter the user can select and unselect.

Sizes, tokens, states and requirements. Values come from the live token package and from Figma: Chip with states.

Behavior

TopicResult
Click on a filter chipToggles aria-pressed and fires chip:change (components/js/chip.js).
Click on a removable chipFires chip:remove; the page removes the chip.
BreakpointOnly the Medium text changes, at 769px.
MotionFrame and surface change with duration-fast-2, ease-standard.
