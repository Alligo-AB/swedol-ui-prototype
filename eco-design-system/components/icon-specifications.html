Icon

Icons symbolize common actions, features and themes in our products. The set is Material Symbols and custom icons: customized symbols, brand icons for Swedol and Tools, social icons and logos.

Naming

Rules for the name of an icon in Figma and in code. They are from the Figma instructions for adding an icon.

RuleExample
Use an underscore _ between words.shopping_cart
Use lowercase, never UPPERCASE, camelCase or PascalCase.chevron_right
Use the Material Symbols name that comes with the icon.icon_name
A customized Material Symbol, or an icon in the style of Material Symbols, ends with _custom.grid_view_custom
A logo ends with _logo.bank_id_logo
A Swedol or Tools specific icon ends with _brand.foot_scan_brand
A social icon ends with _social.instagram_social

Icon item

The item of the Library: an icon at 40px with its name. Hover or focus shows the copy icon and a grey name bar.

PartValue
ContainerA button. Width: fills its grid cell (160px at least).
Copy icon20px, 8px from the top and from the left. Hidden until hover or keyboard focus.
Icon40px, centered, 8px left and right, 16px below.
NameBody small (14/20), 8px padding on every side. Grey bar (surface-navigation-hover) on hover and focus.
HoverWhite, elevation-b-60.
Focus2px border-focus ring, 2px offset.
Grid24px gap on desktop, 16px below 769px.

Motion

None. An icon has no motion of its own. The change from Outline to Fill is made by the component that holds the icon.
