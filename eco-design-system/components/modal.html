Modal

A modal is the most disruptive notification. It blocks the page until the user makes a decision, so use it only when the user must confirm or choose before going on.

Every “Open modal” button opens the real dialog: try ESC, a click on the dark overlay, Tab and the × to close it. Specification: .claude/skills/eco-modal-ecom/SKILL.md.

Variants

    
Modal has no status or emphasis. It varies in size and in whether it carries a label. Choose the size by the amount of content: Small for a simple confirmation, Medium or Large for more.

    
Breakpoint
      

    

    
On mobile (below 640px) Small stays a compact 375px card. Medium and Large fill the whole screen with the buttons pinned to the bottom. Open a real one below 640px to see it.

Usage

    
A modal stops the user. Only do that when the decision cannot wait.

    
The message…Use

    

      
Do
  • Ask one clear question and give one primary action.
  • Name the action on the button: “Delete”, not “OK”.
  • Close on ×, ESC and a click on the overlay, every time.
  • Move focus into the dialog and back to the opener on close.

      
Don’t
  • Use a modal for feedback. Use a Toast.
  • Use it for status in page context. Use Inline.
  • Open one on page load.
  • Open a modal from a modal.
