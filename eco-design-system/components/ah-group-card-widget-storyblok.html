Article Highlight Group Card

Cards that promote articles, campaigns and brands, placed in a grid or in a carousel. A card can have an image, logos and graphics, badges, text, links and a button.

How an editor adds and sets up the AH Group widget (Article Highlight Group) in Storyblok, and the settings of the AH Group Card. The numbers on the screenshots match the numbers in the headings.

Storyblok

AH Group widget

The Article Highlight Group Card is available as a widget in the admin tool Storyblok, for the content managers and content creators of our e-commerce sites. It promotes articles, campaigns and brands with cards that are placed in a grid or in a carousel. In Storyblok the widget is called AH Group (technical name article-highlight-group).

The widget is built from three blocks:

BlockTechnical nameUsed for
AH Grouparticle-highlight-groupThe container. It sets the layout (grid or carousel) and the number of columns or slides.
AH Group Slide Itemarticle-highlight-group-slideOne slide of a carousel. It holds one or two cards.
AH Group Cardarticle-highlight-group-cardThe card itself: image, logos, badges, text, links and button.

The appearance of a card is controlled only by its settings. Different combinations of the settings give cards for different purposes.

Start

Start by adding a section to the page, or pick an existing one, and place the AH Group widget in it. In the section, choose Add Block under Components and pick AH Group in the Insert block list. It is in the folder (3) Organisms.

1. Layout

Layout is required. Choose Grid or Carousel.

Depending on the choice you add AH Group Card directly in the widget (Grid) or AH Group Slide Item (Carousel). The settings that do not belong to the choice are hidden:

LayoutSettings shown
GridGrid Columns, Equal Gutter, Cards
CarouselCarousel Slides, Slides, Title, Supporting Title, Navigation Text, Link

After that the work is almost the same for both. This page starts with Grid. Carousel is described in 9. Carousel.

2. Grid settings

2a. Grid Columns

Choose 2, 3 or 4 columns. That is the number of cards in a row. The default is 4. The number of cards in a row is lower on smaller screens:

The table shows cards in a row per breakpoint. See the breakpoints in Layout.

2b. Equal Gutter

Sets the horizontal space between the rows to the same size as the vertical space between the cards. Useful when there are many identical cards, for example a grid of brand logos.

3. Add the card

Add a card with Add AH Group Card under Cards. A card can be built with many different settings and in many ways. It is the combination of the settings that gives the card its purpose. The settings are described in the next sections.

Fill in Link, the URL of the card, at the top of the card. The button of the card (see 8b. Show Button) becomes available when the URL is filled in.

4. Image

4a. Image Section

Image Section is required. Here you add the image of the card.

  • Image/Video: add an image or a video.
  • Color: the image holder becomes black or white. Useful for example to promote a brand, a cmpaign or any other use of a single graphic element agains a single colored background when you do not want a image background. (see 5c).
  • Hidden: the card has no image holder.

4b. Image Color

Shown when Image Section is Color. Choose Black or White.

4c. Image Aspect

The image can be shown in different crops, called ratios: 3:2, 16:9 or 1:1. Select the card with the ratio you want. In a layout with many cards, an image in 16:9 saves more space than an image in 1:1.

4d. Image Lazyload

When the page loads:

  • Only the images at the top of the page load at once.
  • The other images wait.
  • When the user scrolls down, the images load one after another.

4e. Youtube and Videoly

Add the video you want to show in the image holder. Youtube takes the video ID, for example StBj0vHMesk.

5. Overlay and brand elements

5a. Overlay

Adds a layer on top of the image. Use it to bring out something on the image, like graphics or a logo, or to change the image for another reason. Choose Full, Left or Right.

5b. Overlay Opacity

Sets how transparent the overlay is, from 0 to 100. 0 is fully transparent and 100 is fully black.

5c. Brand Logo

Adds a logo or other graphics on the image. There are two places, and each has its own field:

  • Brand Logo Center: in the middle of the image.
  • Brand Logo Bottom Right: in the bottom right corner of the image.
  • Brand Logo Bottom Right Size: Large or Small. It controls the size of the logo, so that logos of different shapes get a more even feel.

5d. Power Deal

Power Deal Logo and Power Deal Border promote the Power Deal concept. Each has a color, Green or Red. Use the logo and the border together. The content scales with the image format.

5e. News Badge

Switch it on to push or strengthen a piece of news. The text uses our font, so you can change the label to something else if you want. The default is “Nyhet”. The badge has a fixed size for each Desktop/mobile breakpoint (see Layout).

6. Card footer

6a. Footer Section

Switch it on to add content in the footer of the card. The switch turns on more settings further down the settings column, from 7a. Preheadline on.

6b. Footer Padding

Gives the text in the footer padding, that is air around it. Use it in the same way on every card in a group of cards in a section, or in a concept. Do not mix cards with and without it.

6c. Footer Border

The border gives a more premium feel. Use it when Footer Padding is on. Do not use it with text unless the card has enough padding.

6d. Card Size

Large or Small. It sets the size of the text inside the card. The sizes are fixed and adapt to smaller screens: Large gets the same size as Small when the viewport reaches breakpoint-sm. In a layout with many cards, use Small.

7. Text content

The settings in this section appear when Footer Section is on.

7a. Preheadline

Also called eyebrow or pre title. It categorises the content or adds context. It is optional, but use it in the same way in a group of cards or in a concept. Write one item per line. The items are shown next to each other with a divider.

7b. Title

The descriptive headline of the card. It is often the most important part of a card.

7c. Supporting Text 1

Space for body text.

7d. Bulletlist

Write one item per line. There are three kinds of lists: Bullet, Numbered and Checkmark. Use lists sparingly in a card. They take a lot of space, and cards in the same row get the same height, so a card with little content gets the height of the cards with more. A card that leads to a full article or a campaign should be direct.

7e. Supporting Text 2

Space for body text after a list.

7f. Links

Add more links, with or without icons, that help the user to do a task or give context. Add them with Add Inline link. Links Layout is Stacked or Divided. Stacked gives a more separated look. Divided saves space.

7g. Date

Meant first of all to show a date clearly, for example when a campaign ends or the last day to sign up. It can also be used for another short clarification.

8. Background and button

8a. Background Color

Changes the color of the footer of the card, White or Grey. Useful when the cards are placed on different backgrounds.

8b. Show Button

The option becomes available when the URL of the card is filled in. Then you fill in Button Label and choose the type.

8c. Button Type

Choose the container of the call to action: a button or an action link. Use the action link in the first place.

8d. Button Icon Before and Button Icon After

Use icons to strengthen the message or the call to action. A calm icon, like the arrow, helps an action link without taking over.

8e. Full Stroke Border

A border that frames the whole card. It overrides the Footer Border. Use it when the background of the image could blend into the background of the page, for a better frame. Switch it on when the footer border is off, to keep the hover effect on the image holder.

9. Carousel

Choose Carousel as the Layout (see 1. Layout). The cards are then placed in slides.

9a. Carousel Slides

Choose how many cards are shown at once in the first view of the carousel, 2 to 5. The default is 5.

9b. Add a slide

Add a slide with Add AH Group Slide Item under Slides.

9c. Add a card

Open the slide and add a card with Add AH Group Card under Cards.

9d. The card

After that the work is as usual. Continue with 4. Image.

9e. Title, Supporting Title, Navigation Text and Link

A carousel can have its own heading: Title and Supporting Title above the cards. Navigation Text and Link give a link next to the title, for example to a knowledge base.

9f. Two rows

Each slide item can hold two cards, one over the other. Then the carousel gets two rows.
