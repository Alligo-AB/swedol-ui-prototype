Role tier card

A role tier card pair introduces two tiers of one category side by side, for example Standard and Administrator, and links on to the full comparison table.

Tokens, type, sizes, measurements and requirements. The values come from the live token package and from the comparison pages; there is no Figma component.

Width

A pair fills its container. Two cards share the row equally from 769px.

Behavior

TopicRule
LinkSwitches the active tab in the table before scrolling, by calling the table’s own setCompareView(). Call event.preventDefault() so the anchor does not snap mid-scroll.
Scroll offsetScroll to the section with the --header-top-h offset, never a plain scrollIntoView().
Feature stripsThree or four rows taken from rows that really exist in the comparison table and are specific to that role.
OrderThe pairs follow the order of the tabs.
MotionThe link color changes with duration-fast-3 and ease-standard.

Open points: no Figma component. The card is built for the comparison pages, and the link needs their table script.

Accessibility Requirements

TopicRequirement
LinkA real <a> with a label that names the tier. The click handler calls preventDefault() and moves focus or scroll to the table.
IconsThe tier icon and the arrow are decorative: aria-hidden="true".
FocusKeyboard focus shows a 2px ring (border-focus) 2px outside the link.
ContrastText on the dark card uses the inverted tokens (text-secondary-inverted on surface-100).
OrderThe reading order is badge, name, description, link, then the features.
