Components · Notifications

Toast

A toast is short-lived feedback on something the user just did: saved, copied, failed. It slides in, says its piece and leaves without blocking the page.

Tokens, sizes, measurements, states and requirements. Values come from the live token package and from System Toast.

States

Every status in both emphasis levels. A toast has no hover or disabled state of its own: the ×, the buttons and the links have. Its lifecycle (enter, visible, exit) is under Motion.

Behavior

InputResult
Click ×Runs the exit animation, then removes the toast.
Click outsideSame. The listener starts on the next tick so the click that opened the toast does not close it.
WaitAuto-hide after 4 seconds (Success and Informational). Warning and Error stay until dismissed.
TabThe × and any button or link take focus with a visible ring.
Inline linkUnderlined when enabled; on hover the underline goes and the color changes (eco-inline-link, Medium).
Second toastReplaces the first one at once. Only one toast is on screen.

Accessibility Requirements

TopicRequirement
AnnouncementThe toast area is role="status" with aria-live="polite", so new toasts are announced without moving focus (WCAG 4.1.3).
StatusThe icon and the text carry the status, not the color alone (WCAG 1.4.1).
FocusThe ×, buttons and links have a 2px ring, border-focus, on keyboard use (WCAG 2.4.7).
TimingSuccess and Informational leave after 4 seconds; Warning and Error stay until dismissed. Anything the user must act on does not auto-hide (WCAG 2.2.1).
Reduced motionNo slide under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.
