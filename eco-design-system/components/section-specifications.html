Section

A section is a full-width block of a page. Its padding adjusts at each breakpoint and its surface is white or grey.

The tokens, the padding per breakpoint, the measurements and the requirements. Values come from the live token package and from the specification skill.

Width

A section is as wide as the window. The content is limited to 1200px by the side margin.

Behavior

TopicResult
InputNone. A section has no hover, focus or click.
BreakpointThe padding steps at 640, 769, 1024 and 1281px.
Window widthThe side margin follows --px-full, so the content stays centered against 1200px.
MotionNone.

Accessibility Requirements

TopicRequirement
HeadingsGive every section a heading, so the structure is in the markup and not only in the padding (WCAG 1.3.1).
LandmarkA <section> is a landmark only with an accessible name (aria-labelledby or aria-label). Add one when the part is important to navigate to.
ColorNever use the grey surface as the only sign of a change. The heading must also show it.
ReflowThe content must fit 320px without sideways scrolling (WCAG 1.4.10). The side margin is 16px at xs.
Page dividerAn empty div: decorative and not announced. It must not carry meaning.
